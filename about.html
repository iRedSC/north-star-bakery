About us

Our story

We started with a simple idea, make fresh bread and pastries without forgetting the soul. What began as a small neighborhood bakery has grown into a local favorite built around familiar recipes, careful technique, and a lot of early mornings.

How we Source our Ingredients

We keep our ingredients simple and choose quality first. Whenever possible, we work with local and regional suppliers for flour, dairy, fruit and other seasonal ingredients.

Staff Highlights

Our team is small, experienced, and involved in every part of the bakery. From shaping dough before sunrise to finishing pastries and helping customers at the counter, everyone plays a part in keeping the bakery running.
